Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you use our services, whether in-store or online, we may collect personal information that you provide to us, such as your name, email address, phone number, and payment information. For Eastern Rewards members, we collect purchase history to assign points.

2. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to securely process transactions, manage your Eastern Rewards account, improve our customer service, and communicate updates and promotional offers that you have opted into.

3. Data Security

Eastern Bridge Petroleum implements robust, industry-standard security measures, including encryption and strict access controls, to protect your personal and payment data from unauthorized access.

4. Your Rights

You reserve the right to request a copy of the data we hold about you, request corrections to inaccurate data, or ask for the deletion of your account at any time by contacting our support team.
