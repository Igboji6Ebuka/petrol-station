Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about our products and services.

Are all your stations open 24/7?

The majority of our highway and city-center locations are open 24/7. However, some neighborhood stations may close between 12:00 AM and 5:00 AM. Check the Station Locator for specific hours.

What is the difference between Apex Unleaded Supreme and regular Unleaded?

Apex Unleaded Supreme has an octane rating of 98 and includes our proprietary suite of engine-cleaning additives. It is designed to prevent carbon buildup and restore lost power, making it ideal for modern high-compression engines.
